By severity

Mild to moderate
NSAIDs, acetaminophen, caffeine combinations
Moderate to severe
Triptans, gepants, lasmiditan

Not enough relief?

Add a triptan to the NSAID or acetaminophen

No triptans with

  • Coronary, cerebrovascular, peripheral vascular, or ischemic bowel disease
  • Hemiplegic or basilar migraine
  • Uncontrolled hypertension or WPW
  • A triptan or ergot in the last 24 h, or an MAO-A inhibitor

Overuse headache

Simple painkillers ≥15 days/mo, or triptans, ergots, opioids, or combination pills ≥10 days/mo, for over 3 months

When to start

≥4 migraine days a month, ≥4 moderate to severe headache days a month, or major disability

Best evidence

Episodic
Atogepant, CGRP antibodies, propranolol, topiramate, valproate
Chronic
Atogepant, CGRP antibodies, onabotulinumtoxinA, topiramate, valproate

Give it time

8–12 weeks (onabotulinumtoxinA: 24). Not working? Raise the dose first

Could become pregnant

Avoid valproate and topiramate when possible

No US guideline yet

We didn't find a current US guideline on treating tension-type headache. Painkillers on ≥15 days a month for 3+ months meet the overuse threshold

Stop an attack

High-flow oxygen, SC sumatriptan, or nasal zolmitriptan

Prevent attacks

  • Verapamil, the usual first choice
  • Suboccipital steroid injection
  • Galcanezumab for episodic cluster

Not for prevention

Valproate, sumatriptan
